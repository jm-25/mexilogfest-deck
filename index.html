ABOUT
MEXI LOG FEST

Since 2015, over 200 longboarders from all over the world make an annual pilgrimage to Mexico for Mexi Log Fest, an invitational competition that showcases world-class surfers in a format like no other over 7 days of surfing.

This is a celebration of culture, creative expression, connection, and some of the finest longboarding you'll see anywhere in the world.

  • The best and most prestigious surf festival.
  • Top 100 female and 100 male longboarders in the world.
  • More than 30 countries are invited.
  • The top 20 Mexican longboarders are invited.
  • First professional surf event in the world to have an equal prize purse.
  • Highest-paying longboard competition in Latin America (30,000 USD).

More than anything, it's about access—to the surfers, the place, and the culture. That could look like light onsite activation, content captured during the week, or simply tapping into what's already being created.

SAYULITA

Sayulita, a surf paradise, is a renowned destination for surf tourism, attracting surfers from around the globe. With stunning beaches and a vibrant local culture its an ideal environment for Mexi Log Fest 2027.

We are stoked to return to Sayulita’s laid back atmosphere, the birthplace of Mexi Log Fest.
